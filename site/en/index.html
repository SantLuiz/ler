01 / THE PROJECT

Everything you need to keep reading.

[ local ]

Your library is a folder.

Open your EPUBs, TXTs and PDFs, search by name and organize them into subfolders. No accounts, book uploads or telemetry.

[ resume ]

One book can wait for another.

Switch between tabs, bookmark pages and resume from your saved position. In EPUBs and TXTs, select passages and save highlights.

[ at your pace ]

Keys within reach. Time in view.

Navigate with shortcuts, track statistics and estimated reading time. Choose your palette and read in English or Portuguese.

02 / INSTALL

From download to your first read.

Faster startup and a smaller installation. The installer includes everything LER needs; you do not need to install Git, Node.js or Bun.

↓ Download installer · Windows x64

  1. Download and open the installer.

    Choose LER-…-Setup-x64.exe on GitHub Releases and follow the wizard. Installation works offline and requires no administrator access.

  2. Choose where to install.

    Select a folder and integrations: shortcuts, the LER terminal command and “Open with LER” in File Explorer. Keep your books outside the application folder.

  3. Open LER and choose your books.

    Use the Start menu or a shortcut. On the home screen, press Ctrl + L to select your library, then confirm with “Use this folder”.

03 / HOW TO USE

Find. Open. Keep going.

In your library, type a name and press Enter. While reading, these shortcuts will get you started.

BEFORE YOU START

Update without losing your place

Close LER and run the new installer in the same folder. Your data in .ler is preserved during updates. Back up this folder to keep your progress and highlights safe.

Formats and limitations

EPUB and TXT use reflowed text. PDFs are displayed as images, without text search, selection or OCR. DRM, passwords and fixed-layout EPUBs are unsupported. Use a terminal with at least 60 columns and 20 rows.
